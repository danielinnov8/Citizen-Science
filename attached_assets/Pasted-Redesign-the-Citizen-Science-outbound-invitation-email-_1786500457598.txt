Redesign the Citizen Science outbound invitation email system so it feels exceptionally credible, institutional, personal, and understated — like an invitation from a respected scientific organization, university, foundation, or research institution.

The audience includes Nobel laureates, professors, scientists, researchers, academics, and prominent people in science.

The current emails feel slightly too much like automated SaaS outreach. We need them to feel like thoughtful, individually addressed invitations.

DESIGN DIRECTION

Think:
- Nature
- MIT / Stanford institutional correspondence
- Nobel Prize organization
- scientific foundation
- academic journal
- personal founder outreach

NOT:
- startup growth email
- newsletter
- sales sequence
- marketing automation
- flashy HTML email
- promotional SaaS email

The email should almost look like an extremely polished personal email.

Keep the visual design very minimal:
- white background
- black/dark gray typography
- generous whitespace
- no gradients
- no promotional graphics
- no giant logo
- no colorful CTA buttons
- no cards
- no excessive borders
- no emojis
- no marketing badges

If branding is included, use a small Citizen Science wordmark/logo at the very top, approximately 100–140px wide, followed by a subtle horizontal divider.

EMAIL STRUCTURE

Header:
Citizen Science logo / wordmark

Then:

Hi {{first_name}},

I’m Daniel, founder of Citizen Science.

We’re building an independent network designed to make it easier for scientists, researchers, and the public to discover people doing important work across science.

I’m reaching out personally because of your work in {{specific_field_or_contribution}}.

We’ve prepared a preliminary directory page for you here:

View your Citizen Science profile →

{{profile_url}}

If you’d like to participate, you can claim the profile, update your work, and decide what information is displayed.

It would be an honor to have you represented in the community.

Best,

Daniel Innovaté
Founder, Citizen Science
citizen-science.org
{{daniel_email}}

Optional:
“About Citizen Science” as one short, muted footer line:
Citizen Science is an independent platform for discovering scientists, research, institutions, and scientific ideas.

Only include that statement if it accurately represents the platform.

IMPORTANT COPY RULES

1. Every invitation should mention the recipient's actual scientific contribution, discipline, institution, award, or research area.
2. Never use generic hype like:
   - “Your science, amplified”
   - “Join thousands of innovators”
   - “Revolutionizing science”
   - “Exclusive opportunity”
   - “Act now”
3. Avoid exaggerated social proof.
4. Do not say “thousands of scientists are already on Citizen Science” unless that accurately describes active/claimed participation.
5. If profiles were generated from public information rather than created by the scientist, make that distinction clear.
6. Make it obvious there is nothing being sold.
7. Use only one primary link.
8. Display the citizen-science.org domain somewhere visibly in the signature so the destination can be independently verified.
9. Prefer descriptive anchor text like “View your Citizen Science profile” instead of making a long raw URL the primary CTA.
10. Keep total email body under approximately 120 words whenever possible.

SUBJECT LINE SYSTEM

Replace marketing-style subject lines with institutional/personal ones.

Preferred options:

Invitation to Citizen Science — {{name}}
Citizen Science invitation for {{name}}
Invitation regarding your Citizen Science profile
A personal invitation to Citizen Science
Citizen Science — {{research_field}}
An invitation from Citizen Science

For exceptionally prominent scientists, use the simplest possible option:

Invitation to Citizen Science

SENDER IDENTITY

Emails should display:

Daniel Innovaté | Citizen Science

or

Daniel Innovaté
Founder, Citizen Science

Sender email MUST use the citizen-science.org domain rather than Gmail or another unrelated domain.

Reply-To should be a real monitored Citizen Science email address.

FOOTER

Do not use a giant marketing unsubscribe/footer block unless technically required.

Keep footer institutional and understated:

Citizen Science
citizen-science.org

You received this message because we are inviting researchers whose work is represented in the Citizen Science directory.

Include appropriate opt-out language for outbound email compliance, but make it subtle and professional.

TECHNICAL / TRUST DETAILS

Make the underlying email implementation trustworthy as well:
- responsive HTML
- excellent Gmail rendering
- accessible typography
- text/plain multipart alternative
- no tracking-heavy appearance
- minimize unnecessary tracking parameters in links
- links should visibly resolve to citizen-science.org
- no URL shorteners
- no third-party-looking redirect URLs
- use proper SPF
- DKIM
- DMARC
- branded sending domain
- branded return path where supported

The final result should make a recipient think:

“This appears to be a legitimate personal invitation from the founder of a serious scientific project.”

rather than:

“This looks like someone scraped my information and put me into an automated email campaign.”
